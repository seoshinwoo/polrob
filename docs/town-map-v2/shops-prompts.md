# New town map shop sprites

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Original police station and police character were inspected as drawing-style references only; no existing sprite was reused as output.

Camera: elevated direct front, roof roughly 60%, facade roughly 40%, no side faces. All buildings are isolated from ground and vegetation. Runtime resizing uses macOS sips, maximum dimension 768 px, preserving generated alpha.

No huge rooftop mascot, logo, symbol or signage. No readable words, no numbers, no watermark.

Small red and cream striped awning ATTACHED to the front facade above a dark glazed central doorway. One blue-tinted framed window on each side. Modest small cream coffee cup pictogram on a little rectangular plaque centered on the front fascia ABOVE the awning, only about one tenth of total building width. The cup sign belongs on the FRONT WALL, never the roof. Harmonize deep cocoa, warm brick, cream, subdued red, and blue glass. Entire building footprint is rectangular.

## Market transparency corrections

The initial market output had an opaque backdrop. The first built-in edit produced a drawn checkerboard and was rejected. The second built-in edit produced actual RGBA with alpha=0 outside the building. Only that corrected output is used by the runtime. Failed candidates are retained as source history only.

### First correction prompt

```text
Use case: background-extraction.
Edit target: The attached newly generated market building.
Primary request: Remove ONLY every bit of the backdrop and surrounding colored glow. Deliver this same complete market building as a clean isolated game sprite with a genuinely transparent alpha background.
Keep unchanged: exact building shape, teal roof, cream front wall, mustard striped awnings, integrated fruit counters, dark outline, doors, windows, all colors, smooth cartoon drawing style, and current front-facing elevated camera.
Critical transparency constraint: all pixels outside the building outline must have alpha=0. Remove the dark background, beige/teal glow halo, and all cast shadows. Do NOT replace background with black, white, gray, or a checkerboard image. The PNG must contain actual alpha transparency and no background pixels. Cut precisely around the complete silhouette including the rear roof vent and steps. Keep opaque building pixels completely opaque. No ground plane or drop shadow.
Composition: full centered building, preserve aspect and proportions, no cropping.
```

### Second correction prompt (accepted)

```text
Make the background transparent. Remove the checkerboard completely. Return the same teal and cream market building as a transparent PNG cutout, with actual alpha-channel transparency around the building. Do not draw any background pattern, color, glow, grid, or shadow. Keep the entire building unchanged; only remove the checkerboard. Output a PNG with transparent background.
```

Sources: market-generated.png, market-alpha-attempt1.png, market-alpha-final.png.

Final pixels were inspected read-only with Pillow to verify alpha and silhouette bounds. No image pixels were edited with Python.
